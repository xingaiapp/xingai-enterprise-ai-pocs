# Texas Prompt Payment of Claims (Synthetic Excerpt)

**Regulation ID:** TX-REG-CLAIMS-002

After receiving completed proof of loss, insurer must accept or reject claim within 15 business days or state reasons for delay.

**Flood exclusion:** Damage from flood or rising water is excluded under standard homeowners policies; insurers must not treat storm runoff as covered water damage without policy language support.

Escalation required when claimant disputes denial citing ambiguous policy language.
