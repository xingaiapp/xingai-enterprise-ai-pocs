# California Fair Claims Settlement Practices (Synthetic Excerpt)

**Regulation ID:** CA-REG-CLAIMS-001

Insurers must acknowledge claim receipt within 15 calendar days and provide policy coverage information upon request.

Denials must cite the specific policy provision or exclusion applied.

Claims exceeding $5,000 in residential property damage require documented inspection before final denial.

Fraud indicators (frequency, inconsistent statements) require escalation to Special Investigations Unit — not automatic denial without human review.

## Glass Claims

Fast-track glass repair programs are permitted when damage is clearly within comprehensive glass coverage and amount is below policy glass sublimit.
