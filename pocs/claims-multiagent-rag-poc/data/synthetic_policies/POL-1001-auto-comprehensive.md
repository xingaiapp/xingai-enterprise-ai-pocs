# Policy POL-1001 — Auto Comprehensive (Synthetic)

**Policyholder:** Alex Rivera (fictional)  
**Effective:** 2025-01-01  
**State:** California

## Coverage Summary

| Coverage | Limit | Deductible |
|----------|-------|------------|
| Collision | $25,000 | $500 |
| Comprehensive | $25,000 | $250 |
| Liability | $100,000 / $300,000 | — |
| Glass repair / replacement | $1,500 per occurrence | $0 |

## Covered Perils (Comprehensive)

- Theft, vandalism, fire, falling objects, animal contact
- **Windshield and glass** damage when caused by road debris or vandalism

## Exclusions

- **Flood and surface water damage** — not covered under comprehensive or collision
- **Wear and tear**, mechanical breakdown, intentional acts
- Racing or commercial use
- Damage occurring before policy effective date

## Claims Handling

- Glass claims under $1,500 with photo evidence may be fast-tracked
- All claims require incident date on or after policy effective date
- Maximum 2 comprehensive claims per 30-day rolling window triggers manual review

POL-1001
