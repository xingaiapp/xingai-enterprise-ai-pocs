# Policy POL-2002 — Homeowners (Synthetic)

**Policyholder:** Jordan Lee (fictional)  
**Effective:** 2024-06-15  
**State:** Texas

## Coverage Summary

| Coverage | Limit | Deductible |
|----------|-------|------------|
| Dwelling | $350,000 | $2,500 |
| Personal property | $175,000 | $2,500 |
| Liability | $300,000 | — |

## Covered Perils

- Fire, lightning, windstorm, hail, theft, vandalism
- Weight of ice, snow, sleet

## Exclusions

- **Flood, storm surge, and rising water** — excluded; separate flood policy required
- Earth movement, mold (unless hidden water damage from covered peril)
- Neglect and ordinance/law upgrades

## Claims Handling

- Water damage claims require proof of sudden accidental discharge (not flood)
- Claims over $5,000 require adjuster inspection
